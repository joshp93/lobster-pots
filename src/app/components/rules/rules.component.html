Welcome to Lobster Pots. Ye start with {{ gameSettings.openingCash }} pieces of eight (💰), {{ gameSettings.openingPots }} pots (🏺), two working boats and a salvaged large boat that needs repairing before she can put to sea.

Each day, choose which boats to take out, load them with pots, choose whether each boat fishes inshore or offshore, then press "Get Lobstering!". The game ends if ye cannot pay the rent or if ye have no pots and cannot afford another.

The day

The stats bar shows yer money, lobsters caught, date and season, demand, pots, premises and fleet. Use the Boatyard button beside a boat to repair, rename, fortify or upgrade her, or sell her. Use "Buy boats" to see the boats for sale.

Demand

Demand runs from 1 to 10. When demand is high, lobsters sell for more, but they are harder to catch. When demand is low, prices fall and lobsters are easier to catch.

Seasons

Each season lasts {{ gameSettings.seasonLengthDays }} days. Seasons change demand, the chance of storms and how often pots catch lobsters. Summer is plentiful; winter is lean and rough.

Lobsters

Every lobster has a size grade (small, medium, large or jumbo) and a shell (soft or hard). Bigger, hard-shell lobsters are worth more. Inshore grounds mostly produce small and medium lobsters; offshore grounds mostly produce large and jumbo lobsters.

Storms

The forecast sets the chance of a storm and the sort of storm it will be. Calm forecasts mostly bring offshore squalls; evil forecasts are far more likely to send a storm across both grounds. When a storm hits, each pot on the affected ground has a chance of being lost: 30% in a minor storm, 50% in a major storm, and 70% offshore / 30% inshore in the worst storms. Inshore gear is only exposed to the worst storms.

Boats

Small boats carry 4 pots, medium boats 8 and large boats 14. Large boats can be upgraded to carry more pots. Only large boats may fish offshore. Boats take 1–5% wear each day they are at sea and more damage in storms; stronger boats shrug off damage more easily. At 100% a boat is wrecked and lost with her pots and catch. Repair boats in the Boatyard. Rare and legendary premises repair boats for free, and legendary premises can recover wrecked boats along with 40% of their pots and catch.

Premises and rent

Yer premises sets the hard limit on how many pots ye can own. Rent is due every {{ gameSettings.rentIntervalDays }} days. Bigger premises hold more pots, cost more to buy and charge more rent. Three offers appear on the market every 10 days; reputation changes the price ye get for lobsters.

Pots

Pots cost {{ gameSettings.potPrice }}💰 and sell for {{ gameSettings.potPrice / 2 }}💰. Buy and sell pots in the stats bar, then load them onto yer boats each day.

Get lobstering me hearty!
